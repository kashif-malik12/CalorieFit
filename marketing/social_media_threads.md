**Post Text:**

```text
Hey everyone! 👋

If you’ve tried using calorie tracking apps lately, you probably noticed how bloated they’ve become—pushing $80/year subscriptions just to scan a barcode, bombarding you with full-screen video ads, and sharing your personal health/weight data with third-party advertisers.
```

## 💼 LinkedIn Post (Builder / Engineering Angle)

**Headline:**
> Why I built a 100% Local-First Health Tracker with Flutter & SQLite

**Post Body:**
```
In an era where software companies increasingly default to centralized cloud databases and aggressive monetization, user fatigue is real. 

Recently, standard utilities in popular health apps—like scanning a food barcode or tracking a fast—have been locked behind \$80/year subscription paywalls, accompanied by full-screen advertising and heavy third-party telemetry.

I built CalorieFit to challenge this paradigm:
A privacy-respecting, local-first Android application designed with Flutter, SQLite, and Google Health Connect.

Key architectural decisions:
1. Local-First Embedded SQLite: Zero cloud server dependency. All user records, body weight trends, and meal logs live on the physical device.
2. Frictionless Onboarding: No registration screens, no email collection, no passwords. The app opens directly to the diary in under 1 second.
3. On-Device Native Ecosystem: Integrated with Android Health Connect for steps and workouts, Material 3 dynamic theming, and Android home-screen glanceable widgets.
4. Intelligent NLP: Natural language AI meal estimation that parses complex meal descriptions into macros with full manual override control.
5. Global Accessibility: Multi-language support across 7 languages including full RTL layout for Urdu and Arabic.

Building products that respect user attention and privacy isn't just an ethical choice—it's a tremendous market differentiator.

Check it out on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.caloriefit.app
Project Showcase: https://kashif-malik12.github.io/CalorieFit/

#Flutter #AndroidDev #MobileDevelopment #Privacy #HealthTech #BuildInPublic
```
