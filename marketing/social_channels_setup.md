## 💡 The "One-Video-to-Three-Platforms" Daily Posting Workflow (90 Seconds)

When the automated video engine renders today's video (`CalorieFit_Video_DayX.mp4`):
1. **Instagram:** Tap `+` ➔ **Reel** ➔ Select video ➔ Paste caption ➔ Share.
2. **TikTok:** Tap `+` ➔ Select video ➔ Paste caption ➔ Post.
3. **YouTube Shorts:** Open YouTube ➔ Tap `+` ➔ **Create a Short** ➔ Select video ➔ Paste title ➔ Upload.

*Posting across all 3 takes less than 2 minutes and 3x your organic reach every single day!*
