## 📝 Description (Markdown)

```markdown
CalorieFit is an offline-first, private calorie counter and intermittent fasting tracker designed for Android. Built as an indie alternative to subscription-heavy mainstream diet apps, CalorieFit provides complete nutritional tracking with zero ads, zero in-app purchases, and no accounts required.

### Key Highlights:
* **100% Local-First Storage:** All food diaries, custom recipes, and weight logs are stored exclusively in an on-device SQLite database. No personal health data is uploaded to remote cloud servers or shared with ad brokers.
* **Zero Paywalls or Ads:** Features like the barcode scanner, full macro breakdowns, and CSV export are completely free without subscription locks.
* **Integrated Intermittent Fasting:** Tracks popular fasting protocols (14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, and circadian fasts) with real-time physiological ketosis milestones.
* **AI & Natural Language Estimator:** Describe meals in conversational English or capture a plate photo for quick calorie and macro estimates.
* **Google Health Connect:** Seamlessly integrates with Android Health Connect on-device to sync steps and calculate true Net Calories.
* **Home Screen Widgets & Privacy Lock:** Includes Android home-screen glanceable widgets and an optional 4-digit biometric/PIN lock.
```
