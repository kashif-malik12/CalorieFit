Privacy Policy for CalorieFit

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

CalorieFit ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed with a strict privacy-first, local-first architecture. Your personal data, food diaries, body metrics, intermittent fasting history, and meal photos are stored directly on your device in local SQLite storage. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal health data.

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • Dietary & Nutrition Logs: Stored locally on your device. Never transmitted to third-party ad networks.
  • Google Health Connect: When explicitly granted by you, CalorieFit reads step count and exercise burn from Health Connect and writes logged nutrition. This data remains on your device and inside your Google Health Connect ecosystem.
  • Camera & Photo Access: Used exclusively when you choose to take or attach a photo of your meal. Photos remain strictly in your local device storage.
  • Barcode Scanning: When scanning a packaged food barcode, the numeric code is queried against Open Food Facts to fetch public nutritional tables.

3. Advertising & Analytics

CalorieFit contains no third-party advertising and no user-tracking identifiers (no advertising ID tracking).

4. Data Deletion & Retention

You have full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your data to CSV or delete records anytime directly within the app.
