## 🌟 Key Features

- **🔒 100% Local-First & Private:** No accounts required. All food logs, weight measurements, and fasting histories live in on-device SQLite. Zero telemetry.
- **🚫 Zero Ads, Zero Subscriptions:** No paywalls for barcode scanning, macro splits, or history.
- **🤖 AI Meal Nutrition Estimator:** Type or describe meals in natural language (*"grilled salmon with 1 cup of quinoa"*) for instant calorie and macro breakdowns.
- **⏳ Intermittent Fasting Tracker:** Presets for 14:10, 16:8, 18:6, 20:4, and 24h circadian fasts with physiological stage progression (Anabolic, Catabolic, Fat Burning, Ketosis).
- **⌚ Google Health Connect Sync:** Seamless on-device integration with Google Fit and Android Health Connect to read daily steps and active workouts for true **Net Calories** ($Food - Exercise$).
- **📱 Android Home Screen Glanceable Widgets:** Live home-screen widgets for remaining calorie budget, macro progress rings, and fasting countdown timers.
- **🛒 Smart Grocery Shopping Checklist:** Auto-categorizes ingredients into supermarket sections (Produce, Meat, Dairy, Grains) with one-tap diary import.
- **📄 Clinical & Doctor CSV Export:** Export 7, 14, or 30 days of nutrition and compliance summaries to share with doctors, personal trainers, or dietitians.
- **🌐 7 Languages Supported:** English, Spanish, French, German, Urdu, Arabic (RTL), and Hindi.
- **🏆 Gamification & Streaks:** Daily flame streak counter and milestone achievement badges.
- **🔒 Biometric PIN Privacy Vault:** 4-digit PIN lock on startup to protect personal health metrics.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework:** Flutter (Dart) with Material 3 Design
- **Local Storage:** SQLite (`sqflite`, `sqflite_common_ffi`)
- **Health Integration:** Google Health Connect & Android Pedometer
- **Food Data:** OpenFoodFacts API fallback & local USDA dataset
- **Automation:** GitHub Actions CI/CD with automated Google Play Store deployment
